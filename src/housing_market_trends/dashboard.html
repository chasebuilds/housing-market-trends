Chase Builds / Housing Market Trends

Market conditions

Monthly metrics, a time-adjustment rate with a 95% confidence band, and a plain-English read of whether prices are rising, stable or falling.

Comparable time adjustment

Moves a comparable's sale price from its sale month to the as-of month using the time-regression rate shown above (paired resales if the regression has too few sales). Compounded monthly. Ask a licensed appraiser to apply it to a real assignment.

Closed sales are banded by sale price; active and pending listings by list price. Months of inventory = active listings at month end / closed sales in the month. The dotted line on the rate chart is the planted truth and exists only because the data is synthetic. Source and method: github.com/chasebuilds/housing-market-trends (MIT).
